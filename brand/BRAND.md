# Empower Dreams — brand quick reference

Distilled from `Branding Guidelines.pdf` (published May 2026) so content and
image work can conform without re-reading the PDF. The PDF is authoritative.
**This folder is the canonical home of Empower Dreams brand assets** —
update here first; synced copies live in downstream projects (e.g. the
EDSocialMedia repo's `brand/`).

## Typography

- **Headings:** heavy sans-serif (Arial Black, Helvetica Bold, or Inter),
  geometric tracking.
- **Body:** readable sans-serif (Arial, Helvetica, Open Sans), 10–11pt.

## Logo ("Solar Horizon" icon + EMPOWERDREAMS wordmark)

- **Variants:** (A) full-color — flagship, for light backgrounds;
  (B) solid white — dark backgrounds/photo overlays; (C) solid black —
  single-color print only; (D) solid orange — watermarks/merch.
  Files here: `EmpowerDreamsColorLogo.jpeg` (A), `EmpowerDreamsWhiteLogo.png`
  (B). Black and orange variants not yet in hand — request if needed.
- **Clear space:** ≥ the height of the "E" in EMPOWER on all sides — no
  text, graphics, borders, or screen edges inside it.
- **Minimum size:** 120 px wide on screen; 1.25 in (32 mm) in print.
- **Never:** stretch/compress unevenly; add gradients, textures, glows, or
  drop shadows (change the background instead); recreate the old
  crayon/chalk look.
- Wordmark styling: "EMPOWER" in Empower Orange, "DREAMS" in charcoal.
