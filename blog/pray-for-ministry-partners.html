Please Earnestly Pray for Them

As summer winds down and fall approaches, the Lord brought to mind our Empower Dreams ministry partners in India, Uganda, and Sudan — and the incredible personal price they pay to serve Him.

They serve where following Jesus costs something real — in safety, in finances, in so many areas of their lives. And yet they work tirelessly, because they care about every person they might possibly be used to help disciple.

These brave, selfless ministry leaders are the hands and feet of everything Empower Dreams does. We come alongside them from the US — but they are the ones walking the road every day, in their own communities, among their own people.

Please pray with us

  • For their families’ safety and health
  • For their finances
  • For their leadership teams
  • That the Lord would encourage them in their ministry, seeing people saved and discipled
  • That their example would be a blessing to those of us in the US coming alongside them

Amen.

If the Lord leads you to do more than pray, Give Monthly to strengthen the partners behind this work.

Empower Dreams pays no salaries and covers its own operating expenses, so every dollar donated fuels mission impact through our partners.
